Club Wallen Membership — FAQ

The Reserve is a yearly membership for Morgan Wallen fans, available at three levels — The Crew, The Backroad Circle, and The Front Row — with exclusive content, livestreams, member drops, and special experiences throughout 2026.

The Community Livestream is a members-only session focused on fellow members — conversations, music, and community moments. The Morgan Wallen Livestream is hosted by Morgan himself alongside surprise guests. Both are included starting at the Backroad Circle level.

Backroad Circle and Front Row members can submit a request for a direct conversation with Morgan Wallen. Selection depends on availability, scheduling, and approval — it isn't guaranteed for every member who requests it.

Yes — our public shop is open to everyone. Members gain additional access to exclusive limited-edition drops not available to the general public.
